# Computational Biology

## Scientific questions. Real data. Computational tools.

Welcome to a collection of computational biology investigations designed to help students explore biological questions using authentic datasets, statistical reasoning, and interactive notebooks.

These activities emphasize scientific thinking rather than programming for its own sake. Students use computational tools to explore evidence, recognize patterns, choose appropriate analyses, and develop biological interpretations.

## 🌿 Biodiversity, Bias, and Big Data

Explore community ecology using thousands of iNaturalist observations.

Students investigate species richness, sampling effort, community composition, and the strengths and limitations of citizen-science datasets.

[Explore the Biodiversity Investigation](biodiversity/)

## 🌱 Greenhouse Density Experiment

Explore how experimental biologists move from raw measurements to statistical evidence.

Students examine greenhouse data, explore distributions, choose an appropriate statistical analysis, and communicate their results graphically.
